Subscribe once and new or changed events appear by themselves. Calendar apps check for updates every few hours, so changes can take up to a day to show.

  • Outlook: Add calendar → Subscribe from web, and paste the link.
  • Google Calendar: Other calendars → From URL, and paste the link.
  • Apple Calendar or Outlook on the desktop: use the subscribe link.
